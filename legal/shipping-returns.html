PrivacyTermsShipping & Returns
Operational details below are config-driven where available. Confirm fulfillment, eligibility and warranty terms before launch.

Shipping

Fast U.S. Shipping: 2–4 business days. Available regions are currently described as United States, Canada, and selected international regions.

Rates shown in the cart are estimates from central shipping-zone configuration. If a rate cannot be determined, the cart displays “Calculated after location.”

Processing and tracking

Processing times, carrier availability and tracking procedures should be confirmed by the fulfillment team. Delivery dates are estimates and may be affected by location, customs or carrier delays.

Returns

The configured headline return period is 60-Day Returns. Final eligibility, condition requirements, exclusions, refund timing and return-shipping responsibility must follow the company’s confirmed return policy.

To request a return, contact with your order reference and reason for the request.

Warranty

The configured product warranty is 2-Year Warranty. Coverage, exclusions, proof-of-purchase requirements and claim procedures must be confirmed in the final warranty document supplied with the product.

International orders

Customers may be responsible for local customs, duties, taxes or import requirements where applicable. Availability and costs vary by destination.
