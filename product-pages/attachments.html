Attachments explained

Right head. Right moment.

Five distinct contact profiles make it easy to adapt pressure, coverage and feel.

Meet the heads

Ball head

Larger muscle groups and general use.

Bullet head

Focused contact for small areas.

Fork head

Work around calves or either side of the spine.

Flat head

Broad contact for everyday use.

Pairing guide

Head × speed.

Start with the soft ball or cushion at a lower speed. Increase only when the pressure feels controlled, and avoid direct use on bone.

Remember

Comfort leads.

No attachment should feel sharp or uncomfortable. Shorter sessions and deliberate movement are a smart place to start.

Care & storage

Clean. Pack. Repeat.

Wipe each attachment after use, let it dry completely, and return it to the case so every piece stays ready.
